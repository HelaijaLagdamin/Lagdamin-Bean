Our Story

More Than Just Coffee

The Lagdamin Bean began in 2026 with a simple mission: to bring the highest quality, ethically sourced coffee to our neighborhood in a space that feels like a second home.

We believe that great coffee isn't rushed. That's why we partner directly with farmers and roast in small batches here in the shop. Whether you're here for a quick morning espresso or spending the afternoon with a book, we want your experience to be warm, welcoming, and delicious.

Our Values:

  • Ethical Sourcing
  • Community Focus
  • Sustainability First
